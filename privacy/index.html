Privacy Policy

Aura Pulse · Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aura Pulse ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. Your privacy matters to us, and we've built Aura Pulse to collect as little as possible.

Information We Collect

Aura Pulse is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Any preferences you set (such as your sound mix, selected frequencies, breathing settings, or reminder times) are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Notifications

If you enable Mindful Reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you to pause and breathe. These reminders are generated on your device and are not sent through our servers.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We may use standard, privacy-respecting platform services provided by Apple and Google (such as aggregate, anonymized crash and performance reports) to help us keep the app stable. This information does not identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

Aura Pulse does not sell your data and does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Audio content plays entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

Aura Pulse is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Purchases

If you purchase premium features, payment is processed securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Your Choices

You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings. You can clear all locally stored preferences by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at aurapulse.app@gmail.com.
